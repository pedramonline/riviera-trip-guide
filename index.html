Apartment or breakfast hotel?

Compare the final total.

Your ceiling is €240-360 per base for three nights. An apartment at €85/night plus €75 cleaning already reaches €330 before any extra taxes. A €100 breakfast-included room could be the better value.

Look for a whole place or private-bathroom room around Brignole/Foce in Genoa, or the west-centre/port in Nice. Ask about street noise, ventilation, stairs and bag storage.

Nice · local reporting, 17-19 October
The reports do include Nice.
Nice-Matin reports damage at Place Masséna, Avenue Jean-Médecin and Boulevard Jean-Jaurès following 17 October demonstrations, with further clashes on the 18th. Its 19 October live coverage describes dispersal near Rue Trachel around 23:00.
  • Prefer cancellable rooms. Compare western Nice and the port, or a Villefranche base; none is a guaranteed unaffected zone.
  • Recheck on 22 and 26 October, then each morning in France. Ask the accommodation about the actual street and the airport route.
  • If a gathering is active, leave the area and use the coastal fallback; do not walk through it to complete the itinerary.
  • Away-from-centre option: make Villefranche the day’s main outing, subject to its own current transport/access checks.
