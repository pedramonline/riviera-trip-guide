# A slower Riviera

A responsive, interactive Genoa and Nice travel guide for 24-30 October 2026.

Open [the live guide](https://pedramonline.github.io/riviera-trip-guide/), or open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or dependencies are needed. Shortlists, checklist and budget choices are stored only in the current browser; export choices to share between devices.

Research snapshot: 19 October 2026. Hotel amounts are labelled comparison snapshots, not confirmed supplier offers. Transport, events and local conditions must be rechecked before travel. This public edition omits personal names, health information, private booking details and private-source references.

Choose **English** or **فارسی** at the top of the guide. Persian uses a right-to-left layout. Your language is remembered in this browser, and switching keeps your saved trip choices. Share a direct [Persian link](https://pedramonline.github.io/riviera-trip-guide/?lang=fa) or [English link](https://pedramonline.github.io/riviera-trip-guide/?lang=en). Downloads follow the selected language; supplier enquiry drafts stay in English, ready to send.

برای تغییر زبان، بالای راهنما **فارسی** یا **English** را انتخاب کنید. انتخاب زبان در همین مرورگر ذخیره می‌شود و هتل‌ها، بودجه و چک‌لیست شما تغییر نمی‌کنند.
